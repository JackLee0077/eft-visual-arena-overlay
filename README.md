## ✨ Why This Exists

The modern extraction shooter genre thrives on tension — the constant push-and-pull between risk and reward. However, that tension often devolves into frustration when encounters feel arbitrary. This suite addresses that imbalance by offering **deep battlefield telemetry** that respect your time and skill. It was born from a simple observation: information asymmetry should be a choice, not a lottery ticket.

We believe in empowering the individual operator. Whether you're a solo player navigating dense urban maps or a squad leader coordinating multi-pronged assaults, having access to **real-time spatial intelligence** fundamentally changes your tactical decision-making for the better. This project is our contribution to that philosophy.

## 🧠 Core Capabilities

### 👤 Entity Rendering & Classification
- **Hostile & Friendly Tracking:** See all dynamic actors through walls with color-coded outlines based on faction alignment. No more guessing if that shape behind the crate is a threat or a passing scavenger.
- **AI & Boss Highlighting:** Specialized icons for high-value AI targets, distinguishing regular patrols from elite boss guards with unique callout markers.

### 💎 Loot & Resource Visualization
- **Tiered Item Filtering:** Set custom thresholds for item value. The suite highlights only the **premium resource spawns**, clearing visual clutter from low-tier junk.
- **Container Enumeration:** Even sealed containers are previewed, showing you the potential contents of weapon crates, medical bags, and technical supply boxes before you risk opening them.

### 🧭 Extraction & Navigation Beaconing
- **Dynamic Exit Pathing:** The nearest extraction point is always marked with a guiding beacon, recalculating as you traverse the map. Secondary exits are also displayed for rapid alternate routing.
- **Quest Objective Overlay:** If you're running specific mission tasks, relevant objective zones are outlined in a distinct hue, keeping you locked on your goals.

### ⚡ Alert & Notification System
- **Proximity Radar:** A small on-screen mini-radar blips when an unidentified entity enters a custom radius around you, giving you precious seconds to reposition.
- **Threat Direction Indicators:** When engaged, a directional arrow points toward the source of incoming fire, cutting down reaction time significantly.

## 🖥️ Interface & Design

### 🎨 User-Centric Visual Design
The overlay system is built on a **fully configurable HUD framework**. Every element, from visibility distance to color palette, can be adjusted to match your visual preferences and hardware capabilities. We've designed it to be unobtrusive, allowing you to tune the information density from minimal to comprehensive.

### 📱 Responsive UI Across Resolutions
Whether you're playing at 1080p on a small monitor or 4K on an ultrawide display, the interface scales seamlessly. The layout engine ensures all markers and text remain crisp and correctly positioned, regardless of your aspect ratio.

### ⌨️ Gameplay-Preserving Hotkeys
All core functions can be toggled via customizable hotkeys, allowing you to instantly switch between full awareness mode and a "clean" screen for those cinematic moments. Keybinds are fully remappable to avoid conflicts with your existing control scheme.
